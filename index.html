Schuyler[Sky-ler]
Mitchell

is a journalist covering politics and power. She lives in Queens

I'm currently an assistant editor at Mother Jones, where I write about surveillance, civil liberties, and dissent. Before that, I was a columnist at Truthout and an associate editor at The Intercept. I've contributed research to investigative features at Business Insider and New York magazine; projects I've worked on have won George Polk, Livingston, and Silver Gavel Awards.

After graduating from Pomona College in 2020, I got my start in journalism as an intern at Los Angeles Magazine. My freelance writing has appeared in The Baffler, Dirt, The Lever, and elsewhere. I was born in Texas and raised in North Carolina.
